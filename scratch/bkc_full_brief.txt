BINDIR KNOWLEDGE DEVELOPMENT CENTRE INTERNATIONAL (BKC) CAC/IT/NO 56020 REF: BKC/ASA/1.1 DATE: Thursday 27th Aug 2026  Introduction The Bindir Knowledge Development Center International (BKC) was incorporated in 2010 as a community based, non – profit, a Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) and a deliberate Community Development initiative with the following Vision and Mission:  Vision Statement:  To deliberately unleash & encourage active community participation & support in the effective delivery of quality basic education to under-privileged children  Mission Statement: By establishing and operating high quality knowledge & education facilities targeted at basic education on voluntary terms and also by sharing appropriate capacity building experiences  The following are its objectives: 1. Introducing a community initiative on basic education; 2. Providing access to modern basic education tools by children on non-profit terms; 3. Motivating pupils and teachers in primary schools on modern teaching and learning tools; 4. Providing affordable ICT services to the immediate community; 5. Providing a standard model for National Community Support for basic education; 6. Providing a channel for international support to community-based quality basic education delivery; 7. Raising the status of primary school headmasters/teachers through modern initiatives including meeting formats, creating professional networks, conferences, sponsored ICT capacity building programmes etc; 8. Providing educational and entertainment technology Audio-Visual services to pupils and teachers; 9. Providing information on further education and other social opportunities; 10. Providing facilities and platforms for political dialogue; 11. Providing selected commercial services for entrepreneurship development and project sustainability; and  12. providing all other relevant support & services for community development.  The Facilities The BKC has the following facilities fully established: • A well-equipped training/conference hall; • A large and small capacity meeting rooms; • 3 main office spaces • 12 technical services shops/offices;

NATIONAL CONFERENCE FOR PRIMARY SCHOOL HEADMASTERS The BKC will commence special conferences for Primary School Headmasters dedicated to individual Local Government Areas with preparatory activities that include: • Implemented Nationally (in all States of the Federation) • Implemented in individual State by State ((for all Primary Schools in all 774 LGAs) • All Data will be collected and collated (from both Public & Private primary schools) • Full Formal Conferences will be implemented for the headmasters • Each Headmaster will specially be invited and will be given all conference materials (Conference bag, name tag, jotter, pen, souvenir etc) • Special Resource Persons will be sourced to present appropriate technical papers • Each Headmaster will be given a Certificates of Attendance after the conference • There will be a Table Tennis or Badminton Novelty Sports in honour of the headmasters after each conference • Through the conference, there will be strategic drive to encourage the headmasters (Public and Private) to closely partner and benefit from each other’s experiences  BKC readiness for the 21 Conference in Adamawa State (21 LGAs)
